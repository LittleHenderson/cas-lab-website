HELM is in charge

The behavior described below is an intended, unbuilt design, not verified behavior or a demonstrated privacy guarantee.

Every sensor feeds HELM, and HELM holds the controls: which sensors run, when the cube maps, what the light shows, which home devices it controls. Each action goes through the RPM Gate and leaves a receipt. A fact HELM lacks, such as a corner of the room it never measured, stays UNDETERMINED: HELM asks instead of guessing, and never draws a wall it did not measure.

Two lines stay outside all software, HELM included. The camera-on light is wired to each camera's power, and the hard mute is a physical switch. HELM can turn any camera or microphone on or off; it cannot hide that it did. These proposed safeguards are intended to make camera activity visible and controllable; their effectiveness has not been validated.

About

A founder-led lab where research and practical work inform each other.

CAS Lab aims to combine its own AI research with software and consulting work for clients. Questions from real problems shape the research. What the research shows shapes what we build.

The lab intends for AI agents to carry a substantial share of the work. The founder is responsible for direction and for every consequential decision.

The lab is at an early stage. This site marks what is demonstrated, what is in development and what is an idea.

Research interests

  • Cognitive architectures
  • Learning, reasoning and retention
  • Cooperation between systems
  • Useful software
  • Inspectable evidence

What is CAS?

CAS, the Cognitive Architecture System, is Little Henderson's framework for understanding the structure of experience and how ideas become action. It brings thoughts, emotions, beliefs, desires and intentions into relation with the people, relationships, events and circumstances of everyday life.

HELM is the other name at the center of the lab's research. A plain-language definition will appear here once approved wording is available.

Founder

Little Henderson

Little Henderson developed the Cognitive Architecture System framework and the CAS compiler. He is the author of CAS PCBA Engineer, Reality Engineering and Thinking in Cognitive Architecture.

Laura Moreno worked alongside Little at Leo and was a personal inspiration during his work on CAS.

Human accountability

Whatever share of the work AI agents do, a person answers for it. The founder reviews outputs and makes decisions about scope, data, spending and publication.
