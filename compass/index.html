HONEST BOUNDARIES & CLAIM STATUS

What CAS Compass does—and does not—establish.

Customer-production status: OPEN.

  • Research vs. production verdicts Research verdicts do not establish customer-production ACCEPT. Software checks and prototype runs remain bounded to their declared criteria and fixtures.
  • Human decision requirement FLAG_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW requires a qualified human decision. Automated scores and harness outputs never substitute for accountable human sign-off.
  • Strict evidence presence Evidence is present only when a named artifact passes its contract; otherwise it is reported as unavailable.
  • No certification or legal advice No compliance certification, legal advice, or universal safety conclusion is asserted by any score, forecast, diagram, or Evidence Packet.
  • Zero analytics on CAS Lab pages This portal page adds zero analytics, tracking scripts, or third-party telemetry. Google Fonts serves the typefaces; external links open CAS Compass in a new tab.
