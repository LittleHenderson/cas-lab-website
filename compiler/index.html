COGNITIVE ARCHITECTURE SYSTEM · FREE FOR EVERYONE

Make the structure visible.

CAS—the Cognitive Architecture System—is Little Henderson’s framework for understanding the structure of experience and how ideas become action. It brings inner experience—thoughts, emotions, beliefs, desires and intentions—into relation with the people, relationships, events and circumstances of everyday life.

What is CAS?

In CAS, an Idea includes things within and outside the Mind. Mind means the awareness that takes in, processes, stores and expresses Ideas. CAS describes how beliefs shape their meaning, what attracts or repels us, how repetition builds patterns, and how one idea brings another to mind. Its four domains follow principles and values into thought, evaluation and action; its seven drives describe what motivates that movement.

For people exploring psychology, spirituality, sociology, education or their own lives, CAS offers a shared vocabulary for examining situations: how a belief affects a relationship, how a desire becomes a plan, how a group shapes a decision, or how a practice expresses a sense of purpose. You make the interpretation explicit, trace the relationships and explore what might change. Read the framework’s definitions and sources.

The notation and software make this architecture easier to express and inspect. CAS notation writes a model in symbols. The compiler checks supported expressions and produces results and diagrams. CCDL describes connected cognitive components in PCBA models, while CAS-Ohm explores drive, attention and constraint using declared quantities and scales. The lessons connect these tools to human examples you can edit and run.

Recursive Possibility Atlas — why it is here and how to use it

WRITE → CHECK → EXPLORE
Explore a situation and how it could be different.


The Recursive Possibility Atlas (RPA) helps you examine a situation, look inside the ideas that make it up, and explore how it could be different. CAS gives you the symbols to describe it. The compiler checks that description. RPA gives you a map for exploring its parts and, when supplied, alternative descriptions and possible next steps.


Recursive means opening an idea’s parts and examining their parts when more detail has been supplied.


What you can try here today: load Maya’s description, run the compiler, and open the four named parts of that one CAS state. Then load the author’s alternative and compare the descriptions. A CAS state is a description of a situation at a particular point.

3 / OPEN ITS PARTS

What each part means in this authored story


^3 · Noetic operator: Negative
A Noetic operator names a way of relating to an idea. Here, Negative means aversion: “I do not want to hear this criticism.” It does not mean Jon’s comment is false.


^E · Domain: Evaluative
The domain names the area being described. Evaluative means appraisal or judgment: Maya reads the comment as rejection.


_{F4} · Drive: Connection
A drive names the motive. In this story, Maya wants to stay close to Jon.


|f · Register: Affective
A register names the manner of the experience. Affective means feeling-toned: Maya feels hurt.


The code supplies these four labels and their combination. Maya, Jon, the poster, the quoted thoughts and the closing of the chat come from the author’s story. They are not hidden inside these symbols.


Compare an authored alternative
Imagine Maya instead examines what “hard to read” could mean: font size, contrast or wording. She asks Jon which part is difficult to read. The author offers ^6^C_{F4}|A for her examination of the comment.
^6 is Male, the CAS name for ideas composing an idea; here it means unpacking the comment. ^C is Cognitive, the domain of thought. F4 stays Connection. |A is Analytical, a logical manner of examining it. The formal CAS name Male describes a CAS operation, not a person’s gender.
Then repeat steps 2 and 3. The atlas replaces the previous description. It does not draw a proven transition between them. The possible explanations and the question to Jon are authored context; neither expression proves Maya changed her reaction or that the question worked.


The wider RPA design and today’s Flower view
In the full RPA design, the recorded present is the reference point: what the source says is happening now. Looking at another idea changes your view, not that present. A possible next step stays a proposal until a suitable system records what actually happened.
The full nested Noetic Flower is a map of ideas inside ideas, with further possibilities inside each one. This website’s Flower view is a circular layout of supplied descriptions and their named parts. It does not implement that full nested design or generate new possibilities.
The editor walkthrough supports one complete CAS state with four named parts: operator, domain, drive and register. The separate saved software example shows supplied history and a proposed next action. Other CAS programs remain available in the compiler and its usual diagrams.
The story and alternative in this walkthrough were written by the author. The compiler checks their notation; it does not infer a person’s thoughts or turn a story into code.


Three different links: parts, more detail, and possible next steps
The original RPA design separates these questions. A line only has the meaning stated on its label.
Contains part · contains_part
What is inside this description? In the compiler example, the complete state has four named coordinates (its operator, domain, drive and register). Opening a part does not move time forward.
Refines state · refines_state
What is a more specific description of the same situation? This requires supplied records that establish that relationship. Maya’s two expressions are not automatically a refinement pair.
Reachable via · reachable_via
What next state is linked through a named action? A recorded action and a proposed action have different labels. This page does not execute either one. No such link is derived from Maya’s expressions.
These ideas come from CAS Recursive Possibility Atlas v0.1, pages 3–5 and 17. The full design allows a possibility to have its own interior and further possibilities. This website displays only the finite records supplied to it.

Supported input and technical limits
A record is a saved description with its labels and links. The atlas adds one source record around the five compiler records. These six records describe the input; they are not six possible futures.
The editor adapter (the code that converts a compiler result into atlas records) supports exactly one expression such as ^3^E_{F4}|f, in CAS Compile & run mode, with no modules or surrounding whitespace. It derives named part relationships only. It supplies no recorded present, history or proposed actions. Editing any request field invalidates the old atlas.
Imports are bounded to 256 KiB, 128 records, 512 links and a path of 16 records. Full validation limits, source identifiers and file fingerprints are available inside a loaded atlas. A fingerprint checks byte identity; it is not proof of real-world truth.

This compiler makes supported CAS notation executable and inspectable as structures and diagrams. Its checked rules and documented numerical profiles provide a concrete way to explore the notation.
CAS is a cognitive architecture for representing human inner and outer experience: thoughts, emotions, beliefs, attention and intentions, together with people, events, environments and social conditions. It gives you a language for describing how an experience is interpreted, what motivates a response, and how ideas relate to action. 03 / HOW TO USE
Write. Run. Inspect.
  1. Choose a starting point
    Select an example. CAS expressions use CAS; circuit and board declarations use CCDL / PCBA. The example sets the correct language and action for you.
  2. Change the source and run
    Edit the code, then select Run compiler. The first load downloads Python for your browser. Cancel stops an active run. Editing clears old results so they cannot be mistaken for the new code.
  3. Read the meaning, not only the picture
    Inspect Result and Diagnostics. Drag the whole structure to orbit, use the wheel, pinch or Zoom buttons, and select Fit all or Reset view. Switch Drag to One cube for individual rotation. Click a cube for its six faces, or switch to 2D Map. Full JSON retains details that large diagrams may omit.
  4. Save what you need
    Download JSON for the complete artifact, SVG for a diagram, or Interactive HTML for cube controls. The site does not save your edits across reloads; keep a copy of your source.

04 / FAQ
Know what the result means.


Is it really free for everyone?
Yes. This public workspace has no visitor login, subscription, paywall or API key. It runs on your device and needs a modern browser with JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your internet connection and device are your own.


Is this the real CAS compiler?
Yes. The site loads the actual bundled Python CAS compiler, CAS-Ohm helper, CCDL reader and checker, bounded simulators and cube/map renderers. Source hashes are checked when the worker starts. The browser adapter adds file, time and output limits; it does not replace the language with a new parser.
The desktop terminal interface and external native casc executable do not run here. The optional Rust extension is absent, so the compiler uses its own Python fallback. This is the Python compiler edition, not a promise that every manuscript feature is implemented.
Compiler file hashes · Bundled compiler source · Coverage and provenance


Is CAS a chatbot or an AI model?
This compiler reads formal CAS or CCDL source. It does not translate arbitrary English, answer questions like a chatbot, train a neural network or provide general intelligence. No model service is called.


Does “valid” mean the idea is true?
No. It means the implemented compiler checks passed for the selected stage. A false finite assertion can be valid code with holds: false. Recipe yields remain symbolic. In CCDL, a structural PASS is not numerical execution or proof of dynamic stability. Tokens and syntax trees are inspection views, not evidence of correctness.
For CAS, token, syntax and check views follow the upstream full compilation and execution pipeline. CCDL token and parse modes stop at their stated frontend stage.


What can the simulations tell me?
Only the behavior of their explicitly declared models. The fixed-bias scalar CJT profile uses an idealized gain/cutoff/saturation calculation; the independent resistor profile uses typed CAS-Ohm arithmetic on declared coherent scales. Inputs, initial state and a schedule are required.
They are not electronic SPICE, empirical cognition, automatic calibration, general CCDL dynamics or HELM learning. Unsupported components, cycles and missing bindings are rejected.
Read the exact equations and simulation limits


What are the browser and diagram limits?
Source: 64 KiB. Up to 16 supplied CAS modules, each 64 KiB and 256 KiB combined. Simulation input JSON: 64 KiB. Execution: 8 seconds after the runtime is ready. Full result: 4 MiB. Diagram: 2 MiB. Oversized or cancelled runs show no partial result.
Simulations allow at most 256 steps, 128 nodes, 64 components and 8,192 component evaluations, plus each profile’s numeric and trace limits. CCDL also caps statements at 512 and nesting at 32. Device memory may impose a lower practical limit.
The cube renderer shows up to 72 items and 80 relations. Its organizing root and dashed links are presentation only. It uses projected SVG cubes with whole-scene orbit, twist, zoom, pan and individual cube rotation. The parent WebGL perspective camera and idle animation are not included. Unsupported evidence fields explicitly say unavailable.


What do the optional inspection tools do?
Preview inversion compares one selected explicit state using the Noetic, Domain, Drive or compound map. It preserves registers and offers an explicit Copy button. It does not check the full program or apply an edit. A separate limited preview parser is used only for this optional tool; Run compiler still uses the actual Python compiler.
The Recursive Possibility Atlas lets you inspect a retained native example or a supported single compound-state result. Current state, inspection focus and a hypothetical proposal are separate. Flower and Outline show the same data. Source status labels are retained assertions; this browser does not replay a native journal, predict outcomes or operate a live agent.
Read exact coverage and limits


Can my source access files, commands or the network?
CAS module imports can read only the .cas files you supply in this tab. They cannot read your computer’s files or the hosting system. The browser adapter exposes no shell, native executable or arbitrary Python execution. Runtime files load from this site; your compiler inputs stay in the tab.
The host still receives normal web requests to serve the site. This app adds no analytics, code upload or remote compilation service.


Can I reuse the compiler source?
The owner has made this hosted compiler free to use. Public browser delivery does not create a new open-source license or transfer ownership of CAS. No new license grant is added by this site. Third-party runtime components retain their existing licenses.
